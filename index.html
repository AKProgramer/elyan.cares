Professional Boundaries & Client Comfort

Your comfort always comes first

Elyan Cares provides professional therapeutic and wellness massage services only. Every session is conducted with care, respect, and complete professionalism.

Consent-based

Every session begins with your consent, and areas of focus are agreed upon before treatment starts.

Pressure, technique, and focus areas can be changed at any point during your session — just ask.

Strictly professional
